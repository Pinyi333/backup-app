# Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to "rclone backup", a personal tool used only by its owner.

**What it accesses:** only the owner's own Google Drive, to upload and update backup copies of the owner's own files.

**What it collects:** nothing. No data about any other person is collected, stored, shared or sold.

**Where data goes:** files go directly from the owner's computer to the owner's Google Drive. No server or third party is involved.

**Google user data:** used only to perform these backups, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

**How to revoke access:** the owner can remove the app at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

***

隱私權政策：本工具只由擁有者本人使用，只存取擁有者自己的 Google 雲端硬碟來備份自己的檔案，不收集、不儲存、不分享任何他人的資料。
